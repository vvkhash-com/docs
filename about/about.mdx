## Why These Notes Exist

Engineering knowledge decays fastest when it lives only in someone's head or in a closed wiki. Publishing these notes publicly forces rigour — every page has to be accurate enough to be useful to someone other than the original author. You get a searchable, versioned reference; Valeriy gets a forcing function to keep his own documentation honest.
